# Alim, Andrew, Pringle, Brenner (2017) — PNAS 114:5136
## "Mechanism of signal propagation in Physarum polycephalum"

### Discovery
Food stimulus triggers signaling molecule (cAMP).
Molecule is ADVECTED by fluid flow, not diffusion alone.
Flow carries signal AND signal amplifies flow. Feedback loop.

### Mechanism step by step
1. Food detected at tube tip
2. Signaling molecule released
3. Molecule carried by cytoplasmic flow
4. As molecule passes through tube, increases contraction amplitude
5. Stronger contractions = stronger flow = faster signal
6. Signal propagates across organism in minutes not hours

### Speed comparison
Flow-advected: ~50 μm/s
Pure diffusion: ~1 μm/s
50x faster than diffusion for cm-scale organism

### Discrete advection scheme
For edge (i→j) with Q_ij > 0:
  Δc = dt * Q_ij * c_i
  c_i -= Δc
  c_j += Δc

### Rules for simulation
1. Each node stores signal concentration c
2. Signal advected along tubes by flow velocity
3. Signal increases local contraction amplitude
4. Creates response wave visible when food touched
5. Signal decays slowly
